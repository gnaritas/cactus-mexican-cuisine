The story

A Winston-Salem opening nine days from now.

The shop announced a Nov 24 grand opening — the Google listing carries the date, and The Go-To Winston-Salem's November roundup introduced the restaurant to the neighborhood: authentic Mexican cooking, Tex-Mex favorites, and handcrafted drinks.

It's Latino-owned and takes over the space that used to hold Maxburger Burger Bar, right on Old Salisbury Road — a short drive south of downtown.
