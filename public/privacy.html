Privacy — the short version.

This site collects almost nothing. Here is the complete list of what happens with anything you send.

The contact form

When you fill in the form on the contact page, the name, email, phone, and message you typed are delivered to the shop so it can answer you. That's the only reason the form exists. The email you provide is used to reply, and kept only as long as it takes to answer and follow up.

No trackers, no cookies

This site loads no analytics, no advertising pixels, and no third-party scripts, and it sets no cookies of its own. The one embedded item is a Google Maps view of the address on the contact and visit sections — Google may apply its own policies to that embedded view (google.com/policies).

No selling, no lists

Nothing collected here is sold, rented, or shared with advertisers. The message goes from the form to the shop, and that's the whole journey.

Your say

Want a note deleted after it's answered? Reply to the shop's answer and ask — deletion requests are honored.

Questions?

Send them through the contact form — the same form above.
