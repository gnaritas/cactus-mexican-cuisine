The pour

Drinks made by hand, from opening night.

The Go-To Winston-Salem's November roundup introduced the restaurant with three things: authentic Mexican, Tex-Mex favorites, and handcrafted drinks. Two of those are food. The third is a bar — and the “Bar & Grill” in the shop's name makes it official.

What's on the pour list gets revealed with the menu. The quickest way to see it first is the shop's Facebook page, where the opening announcements land.
